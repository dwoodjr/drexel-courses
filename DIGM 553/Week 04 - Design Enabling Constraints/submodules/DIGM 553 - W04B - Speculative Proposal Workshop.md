# Speculative Proposal Workshop

Peer workshopping of the Speculative Proposal. The proposal articulates: (a) your driving curiosity; (b) selected enabling constraints; (c) the matter-network of tools/materials; (d) your Epistemic Positioning Statement ; (e) your Boundary Object.

## Workshop — Before the 1:1s

*~10 minutes. Individual, then brief share-out.*

Read the [[../Documents/Speculative Proposal — Example|Example Speculative Proposal]] (*Did Sun Ra Fear Cryosleep?*).

Write your answers to these three questions about **your own project**:

1. **What is your dog?** — Not your topic. Your specific entry point. The thing you want to follow, determined by your curiosity and experience.
2. **What is your signal chain?** — List your materials, tools, and environments in order. What is the first thing? What is the last? What happens in between?
3. **What does your main tool assume?** — Pick one tool you are using (software, hardware, environment). What worldview is baked into it? Are you working with that or against it?
