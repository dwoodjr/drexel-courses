## Activities

- **(Im)material Potluck** — everyone presents their contribution through the three questions: what does it resist / what does it yield, what does it know, what would it take to put it in a signal chain?
- **Enabling Constraints Workshop** — convert a limitative constraint to an enabling one using potluck materials + the MPR121 sensor
- **Thinking with sensors discussion** — what did the object become when it entered the signal chain?
