# ASN 2: Critical Technical Journal
**Weight:** 25%

---
## Overview

Throughout the course, maintain a journal documenting your making process and reflexive critique. This journal captures all smaller post-class deliverables.

## Three Dimensions
1. **Technical Documentation** — What did you make, what did you try, what broke?
2. **Epistemic Interrogation** — What is your tool thinking? What does your process assume?
3. **Entanglement Mapping** — How are you, your tools, and your materials mutually transforming each other?

## Installments and Embedded Deliverables

### Installment 1 (Weeks 1–4)
Covers course opening through proposal. Includes:
- Journal Entry 0: Potluck reflection (Week 2)
- Journal Entry 1: **Epistemic Tool Audit** (Week 3) — choose one software + one hardware tool; map what each knows, assumes, and forecloses
- Speculative Proposal (see ASN 1)

### Installment 2 (Weeks 5–7)
Deep making phase. Includes:
- Journal Entry 2: **First Impasse Report** (Week 5) — what has resisted; what the impasse reveals
- Mid-term presentation materials

### Installment 3 (Weeks 8–10)
Final development phase. Includes:
- Journal Entry 3: **Process Seed Reflection** (Week 8) — what emerged from the seed exchange
- Final project documentation

## Format
The journal can take many forms: physical notebook, blog, digital whiteboard, PDF, video diary, etc. *Format can be negotiated with the instructor.* Installments submitted as PDF to Blackboard at three specific checkpoints.

## Evaluation Criteria
Depth of engagement, documentation quality, honest tracking of failure/pivot.
