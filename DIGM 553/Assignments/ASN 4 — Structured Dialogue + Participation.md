# ASN 4: Structured Dialogue + Participation
**Weight:** 10%

---
## Overview

Participation in two formal review sessions (Weeks 6 & 9) and the mid-term presentation (Week 7). This grade also encompasses larger class engagement throughout.

## Critique Format: Six Thinking Hats
Critique sessions utilize the Six Thinking Hats format to focus on activating potential rather than rendering judgment:
- **White Hat** — Facts and data: what is actually here?
- **Red Hat** — Intuition and feeling: what does this project make you feel?
- **Black Hat** — Caution: what are the risks or gaps?
- **Yellow Hat** — Optimism: what is the potential?
- **Green Hat** — Creativity: what else could this become?
- **Blue Hat** — Process: what questions should we be asking?

## Evaluation Criteria
Generosity of engagement, quality of critical application, collective thinking.
