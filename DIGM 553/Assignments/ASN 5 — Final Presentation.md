# ASN 5: Final Presentation
**Weight:** 20%

---
## Overview

Render your work public. This 12–15 minute presentation (Week 11) should expose the formative forces of the project.

## What This Is (and Is Not)
This is **not** "here is what I made." It is: here is how the encounter with tools/materials *changed* the inquiry. The process is part of the work. The presentation must make the invisible labor, decisions, pivots, and transformations visible.

## Structure (suggested, not required)
1. **Entry point** — What was your driving curiosity? What was your dog?
2. **The matter-network** — What tools/materials were you entangled with?
3. **Impasses and pivots** — What resisted? What did that reveal?
4. **The work** — Demonstration/documentation of the project
5. **Formative forces** — How did the encounter with tools/materials change the inquiry?
6. **Open questions** — What does this work open that it doesn't answer?

## Format
Recorded presentation with demonstration. 12–15 minutes.

## Submission
Documentation (slides, video, or equivalent) submitted to Blackboard. Revised Speculative Proposal also due this week.
