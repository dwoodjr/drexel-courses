### Scoring Guide
- **Excellent:** Fully exceeds expectations, highly refined.
- **Good:** Meets expectations with minor issues.
- **Satisfactory:** Adequate but missing depth or refinement.
- **Needs Work:** Incomplete or weak execution.
- **Poor:** Minimal effort or off-requirements.
