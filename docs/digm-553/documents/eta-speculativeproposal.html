The Terminus

The sphere is completely dissolved. The vessel contains only water, sediment, and suspended pigment. The Somax2 players have received their last phrase and are waiting for the next input, that may never come. The system holds at a final sustained note, until I stop the transport.

That moment of held-note-waiting is the cryosleep. The instrument doesn't know the journey is over.

The dissolution sphere's swelling phase — the expansion before dissolution — produces micro-pressure events the hydrophone can register. That swelling is a fugitive state: the sphere becoming something else before it becomes nothing.

The buffer threshold default was calibrated for human generated music music. Dissolution events may produce dense and complex fizz bursts with no silence for minutes, meaning S2 never receives a phrase or a pause. The handshake breakdown IS the work of cryosleep: suspended, undelivered, constant, and ephemeral.

The P1 → P2 feedback loop in s2-players is currently OFF by default. This is the most speculative gesture in the patch: each player begins to hear itself through the others, dissolving individual voice into a chorus. Enabling it is a compositional risk worth taking.

DataKnot's descriptor model was built for percussive and pitched musical events (speech, instruments, onsets). It knows how to hear a drum hit. Dissolution is diffuse, broadband, chaotic, often below noise floor. The protocol's memory is of a different sonic world and understanding, one that is for more “standard” and musical.

Somax2 was designed for musical improvisation — human musicians, trained musical corpus, call-and-response at human tempo. Its pattern-matching logic carries the memory of that context. The NASA corpus and spirituals corpus were not what it was built for. That friction is live and difficult to navigate; require some creative modifications.

The bath bomb substrate carries industrial and manufactured assumptions about dissolution and the use of earthen material for selfcare. Relaxation, luxury, self-care. The sphere's chemistry (citric acid + baking soda) is commercially standard. What it does NOT know: that the water it enters is not meant for relaxation; it is the Atlantic, carrying centuries of trauma.

Is the gap between what dk.onset~ registers and what the sphere carries compositional content — or a limitation to work around?

Sensory Worlding

The hydrophone is cold and fragile held in water. The piezo disc pressed into epoxy is an instrument meat to monitor the d
body in solution. It understands the water's materiality before it hears it.

The OSC bus is invisible. The DataKnot onset trigger fires silently via it representation of reality. There a moment the descriptor value crosses a threshold.

Max's GUI enforces a particular aesthetic of legibility — patch cables, number boxes, toggle states. It wants you to see the logic. But the dissolution event is happening in the vessel; what the GUI shows is a translation two steps removed.

The DIY hydrophone epoxy seal is somewhat uncertain, it could fail in extended immersion. Recipe A produces loud bubbling hydrophone events they may saturate the ADC input. Both “failures” are materials of the work to be navigated.

The pitch confidence gate (> 0.45) silently discards dissolution sounds it deems 'too noisy' or out of time. What it's discarding is certain sounds of the structural breakdown. Staying with the trouble here means lowering the confidence floor, and letting more uncontrollable noise in.

The Dog

"I am obsessively inquiring into the gap between the material dissolution of an object in water and Somax2's representation of that dissolution, because the system can hear a fizz event and respond to it, but it cannot hear the the events of the Middle Passage long past. Worldview Statement

This apparatus believes the world is made of detectable events — discrete onsets, pitch values above a confidence floor, phrases bounded by silence. It was built to catch things that arrive cleanly and respond with pattern in kind. What the Middle Passage produced; bodies that didn't arrive, voices that dissolved before they could be heard, signals lost in the water. All of these fall structurally below the threshold. The work attempts to performs it.

Organic Memory Triptych — Movement II

The apparatus is the instrument. What does the signal chain think the world is? And what does it foreclose about how a Black body moves through water, time, and space?

The pitch confidence gate is currently set at 0.45. it is discarding some of the structurally interesting dissolution sounds. What is lost in that handshake?

What happens to Somax2's pattern-matching when the corpus is NASA + spirituals + dissolution? Does it make coherent phrases or noise?

Technical Constraint
No audio playback sourced from outside what the “built” vessel (the material and its sensors) perceives (no additional instruments). All sound-making events must originate from the dissolution event itself. Somax2's responses are the only sonic addition, and they must emerge from the corpus of selected.

Why: Forces the chemical rate of the sphere to set the compositional tempo. Prohibits me from 'filling in' compositional gaps with too much pre-composed musical material.
Forecloses: Pre-recorded ambience, drone bed as safety net.

Material Constraint
Each performance uses a new, unrepeatable sphere. No sphere is used twice. Molds can be reused but each cast is unique in its material composition specifics.

Why: The sphere is a body. Bodies are not reusable instruments. Each dissolution is singular and irrecoverable. This is the temporal logic of the Middle Passage, not the loop logic of sample-based music.
Forecloses: Repeatability, A/B testing of the same sphere, 'standardized' conditions.

Enabling Constraints

Affective Constraint
During performance, I do not adjust the signal chain once the sphere enters the water. Whatever the apparatus registers, it registers. Whatever it fails to register, it fails to register.

Why: Agre's split identity, specifically: the critical foot cannot intervene during the material event. The critique is written here, in this document (and will be in the subsequent reflections). In performance, the making foot holds still.
Forecloses: Live patching, threshold adjustment, any real-time rescue of 'bad' data.

The Boundary Object

What the committee needs to see: Evidence that the apparatus is functional and intentional. A signal chain that works. Sensor-to-synthesis mappings that are documented and defensible. A compositional logic that can be articulated in prose via a research design framework.

What the practice needs: A dissolution event that doesn't resolve. A Somax2 response that can't catch what's happening in the water, but still make musical sense enough of it to respond. A performance that demonstrates the epistemological limit of pattern-recognition as a model for dissolved and disparate memories.

The tension: The committee wants a working system. The work requires a system that fails in specific and meaningful ways. The proposal is to build both: the functional apparatus AND the documented failure as compositional and theoretical content.

Epistemic Positioning Statement

Max/MSP wants me to be a coder , a builder of logic, a designer of routing systems. Somax2 wants me to be a performer, someone who provides “influence” and receives “response” in feedback. DataKnot wants me to be an analyst, someone who extracts features from audio signals.

None of these roles accounts for what I am: a Black practitioner asking a Black speculative question about a technology of suspension that the apparatus cannot alone theorize. My body is not legible to the pitch confidence gate. The Middle Passage is not in the Somax2 corpus.

The epistemic position I'm working from is not against the tools, it is attempting to negotiate the friction between what they know and what I'm bringing them. The gap between what dk.onset~ detects and what the dissolution sphere carries is part of the dissertation site.
