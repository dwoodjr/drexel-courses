About the Course

DIGM 553 is a graduate-level studio course in which students experiment with emerging tools and techniques, applying their creative vision through a research-creation methodology. This course encourages risk-taking and exploration — the process of making is the inquiry.

Key themes:

  • Research-Creation — critical inquiry and creative production as co-constitutive
  • Epistemic Tools — tools are not neutral; they encode worldviews
  • Entanglement — practitioner, tool, and material are mutually constituted
  • Enabling Constraints — productive limitation as generative force
  • Physical-Digital Entanglement — sensors, microcontrollers, and software environments
