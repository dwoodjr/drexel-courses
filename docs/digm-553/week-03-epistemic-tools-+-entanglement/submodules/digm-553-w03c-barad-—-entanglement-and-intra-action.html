Barad — Entanglement and Intra-action

Barad asks us to reconsider a pretty basic assumption: that things exist before their relations.

i.e.:

  • That you are one thing,
  • Your tool is another thing, and
  • Your material is yet another thing
    And then those things interact. They say no.

The boundaries between things are not given.
They are enacted through practice and relation.
This is what is meant by intra-action rather than interaction.

Interaction vs. Intra-action

Interaction assumes two pre-formed entities coming together: you + Touchdesigner (TD), each fully formed, exchanging influence.

Intra-action says: the entities emerge through the relation. The version of you that exists when you're deep in a patchwork is not the same version of you that existed before. The version of TD that exists in your practice is not the same as TD in the abstract. The practice/relation constitutes both.

Apparatus

Barad uses the word apparatus for the whole material-discursive arrangement through which knowledge gets made. An apparatus includes the social, institutional, and conceptual structures that make a particular kind of inquiry possible (through the faculties of the object). The lab, the protocol, the grant structure, the assumptions of the field, all apparatus.

For this course: your apparatus includes your hardware, your software, your studio space, your practice context, the course itself, the readings, your collaborators. That is because they are all co-constituting the phenomena you're making.

Industry Pulse — Entanglement at Scale

Bluecadet builds for institutions: museums, cultural centers, public spaces. Their work is entangled with architecture, with curators, with conservation concerns, with visitor behavior at scales from one person to thousands. The "tool" is never just software; it's a whole apparatus including the wall it is mounted on and the demographic of who walks past it.

Meow Wolf is an interesting case: they're a collective that became a company. Something changed in the apparatus. The entanglements shifted: new stakeholders, new financial structures, new obligations. What did that do to what was possible to make? This is Barad's point made very concrete.

BRDG studios works with clients communities where the entanglement is highly relational. The apparatus includes trust. It includes narrative and creative vision. It includes whether people in that room feel the purpose and story by what was built. That's its in the apparatus.

The Practical Consequence

If you are entangled with your tools, mutually constituted by them, then the tool audit is a map of the conditions of your own practice. You're not standing outside your tools observing them. You're in it. The audit is a way of trying to see something you're inside of.

That's hard. It is supposed to be hard.
