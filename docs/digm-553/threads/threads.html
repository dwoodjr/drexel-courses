Conceptual Threads — DIGM 553

Conceptual Threads

These eight threads run across all 11 weeks. They are not topics that appear once and disappear — they are recurring structures that deepen each time they're encountered in a new context. Each one is a lens you can hold up to your project at any point in the semester.

"The point is not to know in advance where you will arrive, but to follow the thread."

The Threads

Research-Creation
36

Making and thinking are not sequential — they are co-constitutive. Research-creation names the activity at the prebifurcation point, before inquiry and practice diverge into separate institutional logics. The methodology running beneath everything else in this course.

Epistemic Tools
14

Every tool encodes a worldview. When you pick up an instrument, you adopt a set of propositions about what the world is and what can be known about it. This thread asks: what does your tool claim — and are you making that claim consciously?

Enabling Constraints
20

A limitation that generates emergence rather than just restriction. Gravity enables the jump. The 12-point sensor grid enables a particular kind of sculptural thinking. This thread asks: what is the co-cause you're pushing against, and is it working for you?

Entanglement
26

Practitioner, tool, and material are not separate entities that interact. They are mutually constituted through intra-action — through the practice itself. Barad and Camus & Vinck give this claim its theoretical and material grounding.

Failure as Method
26

Failure and impasse are data, not obstacles. Agre's concept of the hassle — the recurring friction that reveals structural assumptions — is the engine of the Critical Technical Journal. This thread tracks how your dead ends become your research findings.

Physical-Digital Entanglement
21

The signal chain running from body to sensor to microcontroller to software to output is a series of transformations, not a transparent pipeline. This thread follows what is amplified and reduced at each step — and asks what creative work happens in those gaps.

Making Public
15

Rendering work public is a practice, not a delivery. Loveless and Haraway: research-creation carries an obligation to make formative conditions visible, not just results. This thread tracks how your work encounters audiences and what it asks of them.

Collective Practice
21

The studio is a site of collective becoming. Critique is activation, not judgment. Manning & Massumi: the seminar event works when the potential of what might occur isn't pre-reduced to conclusions already reached. This thread tracks how individual practices meet and change each other.
