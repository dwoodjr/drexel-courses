Blast Theory

Can You See Me Now? (2001)

Urban space as game board. Being seen, or not, as the actual stakes. What happens when players on the internet and runners in a physical city occupy and threaten each other in the same space?

Physical location + network presence → GPS coordinates, player proximity, connection status

Being caught = your GPS position and a runner's GPS position intersect. Presence is literally data. Absence is too.

GPS receivers (worn by street runners) + handheld computers + walkie-talkies + networked server
↓
Custom real-time game engine + live mapping (Mixed Reality Lab, Nottingham)
↓
Mixed-reality chase: online players as avatars; runners as live GPS dots in the city
